About the Practice

A focused practice, close to each client.

Iandara Alves Sociedade Individual de Advocacia was established in Brasília in January 2024 as an independent legal practice serving individuals and small businesses across the Federal District.

The practice is organized as a Sociedade Unipessoal de Advocacia under Brazilian law, which means clients work directly with the attorney responsible for their case from first meeting to final resolution. There is no handoff to junior associates and no impersonal case queue.

Our focus is on doing a small number of things well: reading each matter carefully, communicating clearly, and moving through the necessary steps with steady, deliberate work rather than promises of shortcuts.

Areas of Practice

What we work on.

— 01

Civil Matters

Everyday legal questions involving family, consumer relations, real estate transactions and general civil disputes. We review the situation, explain the options in plain language and act as required.

— 02

Contracts & Drafting

Review, drafting and negotiation of contracts for individuals and small businesses — service agreements, lease contracts, partnership documents and commercial arrangements.

— 03

Corporate & Small Business

Legal support for entrepreneurs and small companies: entity formation, ongoing corporate housekeeping, commercial disputes and day-to-day advisory work for growing operations.

— 04

Consumer Rights

Representation in disputes involving purchases, services and consumer protection matters. We assess the situation, engage the counterparty and pursue the appropriate remedies.

— 05

Preventive Advisory

Reviewing arrangements before they become disputes: reading contracts before signing, checking obligations before renewing, and helping small businesses avoid predictable legal problems.

— 06

Second Opinion

A structured review of matters already being handled elsewhere, when a client wants an additional perspective on strategy, documents or the state of an ongoing case.

How We Work

A simple, direct way of working.

i

Initial Review

We read the matter carefully before quoting fees or recommending steps. No obligation to proceed.

ii

Clear Scope

Written engagement letter setting out what is included, what is not, and how fees are calculated.

iii

Direct Contact

Clients speak with the attorney responsible for the case. No layers to reach the person doing the work.

iv

Regular Updates

Status updates at agreed intervals — and immediately when a decision from the client is required.
